Game sounds (move_1-3, place, pick, crumble_1-2)
    Built from recordings in "75 CC0 breaking / falling / hit sfx" by rubberduck (CC0),
    https://opengameart.org/content/75-cc0-breaking-falling-hit-sfx
    The wood knocks and rock sounds were pitch-shifted, filtered, trimmed and layered (with a synthesized
    scrape before each knock); no other changes to the recordings.

Win and lose jingles (win, lose)
    Synthesized for this game (additive horn-like voices, a drum thump and a little reverb). No samples.
