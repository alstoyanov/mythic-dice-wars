## Logo

misspell text. Use Cinzel Decorative Bold (already in Tile Shifter's fonts, OFL) with a gold bevel, and a small die in
place of the dot on the "i" of "Dice". Save as `assets/branding/logo.png` (transparent, 2048 px wide).
