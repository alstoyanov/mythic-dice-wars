# Dice towers

The battle animation: dice drop into the attacker's tower (left) and the defender's tower (right), tumble down and
land in a tray. Every tower is drawn **front-on and upright** (not isometric) and comes in three parts, so the game
can draw the dice between them:

1. `tower.png`: the tower body.
2. `tray.png`: the tray the dice land in.
3. `tray_front.png`: only the front lip of the tray, drawn over the dice.

- **One conversation per pantheon**: paste the three prompts in order.
- Generate the tower at **1024 × 2048** (portrait) and the tray and lip at **2048 × 1024**.
- Save as `assets/towers/<pantheon>/<file>`.
