# Music (Suno)

Each pantheon gets a calm **menu** loop and a tense **war** loop. There are also short victory and defeat stings.

How to generate each track:

1. In Suno, choose **Custom** and turn **Instrumental** on.
2. Paste **Style of Music**, **Exclude styles** (under *More options*) and **Title** from the blocks below.
3. Under *More options*, set **Weirdness** to about 25% and **Style influence** to about 75%.
4. Make 3–4 versions and keep the one with the **steadiest middle**. Download it as **WAV** and cut a seamless loop from the middle.
5. Save it as `assets/audio/music/<file>.ogg`.
