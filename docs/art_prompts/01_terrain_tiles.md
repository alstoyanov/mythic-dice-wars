# Terrain tiles

The ground of every cell on the map, and the cliffs on the sides of the island. The game tints the ground tiles with
the owner's colour, so they are deliberately **pale and low in contrast**.

- **One conversation per realm**: paste that realm's prompts in order, top to bottom.
- Generate at **2048 px** wide. The white is cut away and the tiles scaled down by a step to be added to
  `tools/prepare_assets.py` when the first tiles arrive (the same method it already uses for the dice).
- Diamond tiles are for the **Isometric squares** board, hexagon tiles for the **Hexagons** board. Cliffs serve both.
- Save as `assets/terrain/<realm>/<file>`.
