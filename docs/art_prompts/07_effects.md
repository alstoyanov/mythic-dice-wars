# Effects (sprite sheets)

Short animations for battles and miracles, each as one row of eight frames.

- **One conversation for all of them**, in order.
- Generate at **4096 × 512** (eight square frames side by side).
- Save as `assets/vfx/<file>`. (Particle effects made in Godot can replace any of these.)

### `dust.png`

```
A sprite sheet of eight animation frames in one horizontal row of equal square cells with white gaps between them, on a plain pure white background: a puff of grey-brown dust bursting outward and fading away, from the first frame to the last. Hand-painted game-effect style with a bright core and soft edges, the same size and position in every frame. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signature, no border, no frame.
```
